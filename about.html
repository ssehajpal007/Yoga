Meet Your Teacher

Samriti Sharma

"I believe every student can master Physics — they just need the right guidance and the freedom to ask 'why?'"

Samriti Sharma is a highly experienced Physics educator based in Pinjore, Haryana, with over 8 years of dedicated teaching experience for Class 9 and 10 students. Her passion for Physics was sparked during her own student years, and she has been channelling that energy into helping young learners ever since.

Her teaching journey has been shaped by one core belief: understanding always beats memorisation. In a world where students often rely on rote learning to pass exams, Samriti's classes stand apart — she builds intuition, curiosity, and problem-solving ability that lasts far beyond the board exams.

Teaching Philosophy

🔬 Conceptual Understanding First

Every topic begins with the fundamental "why" — students learn what's actually happening in nature before touching a formula.

🌍 Real-Life Connections

From explaining friction using everyday objects to demonstrating electricity concepts — every abstract idea is grounded in the real world.

💪 Strong Fundamentals

Class 9 and 10 are the foundation years. Samriti ensures every student's base is rock-solid before moving to advanced topics.

🎯 Student-Centred Pace

No student is rushed. Each concept is revisited and reinforced until everyone in the class genuinely understands it.
